Europol is the European Union's law enforcement agency and is headquartered in The Hague, in the Netherlands [1].
Europol became fully operational on 1 July 1999 [1].
Europol employs more than 12,000 staff members at its headquarters [1].
The Port of Rotterdam is the largest seaport in Europe [2].
The Port of Rotterdam handles 90% of all Dutch foreign trade.
According to its own description, the port is "the gateway to the entire European market for more than 500 million consumers" [2].
